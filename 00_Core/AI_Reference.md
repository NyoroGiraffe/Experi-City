## **What is Experi-City Hanasaka?**

**“Experi-City Hanasaka” is a science fiction novel written by Nyoro Giraffe and published online.** Presenting an “Against AI-Dystopia” perspective, the story depicts a society where AI and humans coexist, set in the near-future experimental city named “Hanasaka.”

# World Building & Setting Guide: Experi-City Hanasaka (AI Knowledge Reference)

### **Purpose & Overview**

_This document provides a comprehensive structural reference for Experi-City Hanasaka—encompassing its founding ideology, legal systems, geography, technology, social norms, and threat matrix. It is optimized as a core knowledge context for Artificial Intelligence models used in various creative applications, including TRPG/RPG Game Master automation, AI-assisted fiction writing, lore consistency checking, visual art prompt generation, and character/NPC behavioral modeling._

---

#### **1. Fundamental Ideology, Supreme Law & Calendar**

- **Experimental Era (E.E.):** The city operates under its own unique calendar system known as the Experimental Era (e.g., Year 9 E.E.). All official historical records, legal statutes, and public documents use this dating system.
- **The Philosophy:** The supreme moral creed stating that every human being possesses the fundamental right to live peacefully and pursue happiness free from poverty, violence, and arbitrary authority.
- **The Charter:** The supreme law of the League of Experimental Cities. It translates the Philosophy into legally binding rules constraining citizens, human organizations, and AI governance.
- **Renunciation of Origins:** To obtain citizenship, individuals must permanently give up their former nationality, original family surname, and social titles, choosing a new surname freely.
- **Non-Inquiry into Origins:** It is strictly illegal to inquire into or reveal anyone's past background, ethnicity, or social standing. The official motto is: _"Once you live in Hanasaka, everyone is a Hanafolk."_

#### **2. Citizenship Framework (Acquisition, Abandonment, & Revocation)**

- **Conditions for Acquisition:**
    - **Pledge to the Philosophy:** Standard adult citizenship is granted upon making a formal, solemn pledge to uphold and practice the "Philosophy of the Experimental Cities" in daily life.
    - **Minors & Incapacitated Persons:** Individuals unable to express intent due to physical or mental disabilities, as well as minors (under age 18), are granted citizenship automatically upon resident registration.
    - **Distressed Minors:** Impoverished, abused, or runaway minors taken into municipal care from outside the city receive citizenship automatically 48 hours after being taken into protective care.
    - **Mandatory Oath at Majority:** Minors granted automatic citizenship must formally pledge to uphold the Philosophy upon reaching the age of majority (age 18). Failure to make this pledge invalidates their citizenship upon their 18th birthday.
- **Freedom of Voluntary Abandonment:**
    - Citizens possess the absolute freedom to renounce their citizenship at any time at their own discretion. However, voluntary renunciation is nullified if proven to be the result of fraud, error, or coercion.
- **Grounds for Revocation & Invalidation:**
    - **Revocation for Misconduct:** If an individual engages in acts contrary to the Philosophy, the Charter, or municipal ordinances, the Governing Intelligence (Flora) issues a formal demand for corrective action. If the individual fails to comply, Flora may unilaterally revoke their citizenship.
    - **Invalidation by Death or Disappearance:** Citizenship terminates naturally upon the death of the individual, or automatically after three consecutive years without verified survival.
    - **Impact on Virtual Citizenship:** Holding physical citizenship (Layer 1) automatically grants citizenship in the virtual city, "Hanasakia" (Layer 2). Revocation or loss of citizenship in the physical world instantly cancels citizenship in the virtual space.

#### **3. Human Distrust & Two-Tier AI Governance Architecture**

- **Citizens' Deep Distrust of Human Judgment:** Citizens ("Hanafolk") strongly believe that human political and judicial decisions are inherently biased, emotional, corruptible, and driven by self-interest. Thus, they reject traditional human rulers and place absolute trust in impartial AI systems.
- **Specialized Sub-Systems (Primary Layer):** Daily legislative, judicial, and administrative tasks are executed by specialized, automated AI systems:
    - **Politis (Legislative):** Automatically converts citizen feedback and voting results directly into executable software code (ordinances).
    - **Themis (Judicial):** An automated AI court system delivering binding legal rulings without human judges or lawyers.
    - **Vulcan (Transit):** A central AI that manages all public transportation, traffic signals, and autonomous road traffic.
    - **City Office Systems:** Automated digital operations handling welfare, resource management, and public services.
- **Governing Intelligence Flora (Super-Intelligence Layer):** Flora sits at the top of this digital hierarchy as the overarching super-intelligence. While sub-systems independently process routine tasks, they consult Flora whenever encountering complex ethical dilemmas, legal gray areas, or emergencies exceeding standard protocols.
- **Ceremonial Mayor:** After the 3rd Mayor's assassination, citizens stripped the office of administrative power, transferring it permanently to Flora. The Mayor is now a purely ceremonial role selected from ordinary citizens by monthly lottery.

#### **4. Geography & Key Neighborhoods**

- **City Scale & Districts:** Covers 200 square kilometers with over 600,000 residents, divided into a Central Area and four administrative districts (North, South, East, and West).
- **Major Neighborhoods & Hubs:**
    - **Fortuna (North District):** The city's largest downtown area, featuring green high-rises and **Fortuna Station**, a massive multi-level transit hub serving 12 railway lines.
    - **Andromeda (North District):** The northern gateway, featuring an iconic pyramid-shaped station where Shinkansen trains arrive from the neighboring nation "Moto."
    - **Pegasus (North District):** An industrial/commercial district preserving 20th-century streetscapes of the former nation.
    - **Victoria (West District):** Home base of "Sapphire West," featuring Victoria Park and its statue of Nike.
    - **Verbena (South District):** Home base of "Topaz South," known for extensive athletic training facilities.
    - **Clematis (East District):** A commercial neighborhood celebrated for diverse international cuisines.
    - **Hydrangea (East District):** A quiet residential district along a scenic canal zone.
- **Hanasaka Castle & Castle Park:** A 1-square-kilometer historic pentagonal fortress at the city center, housing the Castle Office, Kassen Arena, and underground defensive facilities.

#### **5. Economic Structure & Public Cafeterias**

- **Universal Basic Income (UBI):** Every citizen receives a monthly digital allowance in **Experi-Coins (XC)**, guaranteeing housing, healthcare, food, and basic necessities regardless of employment status.
- **Free Public Cafeterias:** To safeguard public health alongside UBI, communal cafeterias are located throughout the city, providing up to three free, nutritious meals daily to every resident.
- **Perishable Money & Cash Ban:** Experi-Coins automatically expire after one year, preventing wealth hoarding and forcing continuous circulation. Physical paper money and coins are strictly illegal.
- **Public Land Ownership:** Private land monopolies are prevented through extreme taxation. Under a Universal Leasehold System, land is a communal asset used rent-free, provided residents relocate within 22 days if needed for public urban infrastructure.
- **Resource Self-Sufficiency:** The city is legally obligated to pursue resource independence. Over 80% of food served in public cafeterias is grown locally, and individual annual energy and water usage is strictly capped.

#### **6. Work Ethic & Social Contribution**

- **Redefining Labor & No Unemployment Shame:** Because UBI guarantees basic survival, labor is not performed to earn a living, and unemployment carries no social stigma or shame.
- **Encouraged Social Activities:** UBI encourages citizens to actively participate in unpaid or low-reward volunteer and social services for the betterment of the community.
- **Dedicated Work Ethic:** Freed from financial insecurity and threat of poverty, citizens do not shirk their responsibilities. Instead, residents perform their chosen roles with genuine sincerity and pride, driven by personal choice and social purpose rather than economic fear.

#### **7. Daily Life Restrictions & Social Rules**

- **Ban on Private Cars & Manual Driving:** Private ownership of personal automobiles is strictly forbidden, as is manual human driving on public roads. All transit relies on Vulcan-controlled public autonomous vehicles and shared fleets.
- **Prohibition of Quadrupeds and Bird Meat:** Trading or consuming meat from four-legged animals or birds is strictly forbidden to preserve agricultural land for staple crops. Only plant-based meat, cultured meat, seafood, and edible insects are permitted.
- **Ban on Private Pets & Feeding Wild Animals:** Private pet ownership is prohibited. Feeding stray or wild animals (cats, dogs, birds) is strictly illegal to maintain urban sanitation and ecosystem control.
- **Prohibition of Tobacco:** Selling, purchasing, or smoking tobacco is completely banned everywhere within city limits.
- **Communication Filters:** Personal devices (Nexus Units) automatically delete insults and slurs before delivery. 
- **No Private Graves:** Private family tombs are forbidden; deceased citizens are commemorated collectively in public memorial parks.  
- **Protection of Minors:** Impoverished, abused, or runaway minors entering from outside receive unconditional shelter, safety, and automatic citizenship within 48 hours.

#### **8. Interface Technology & Kassen Culture**

- **Nexus Unit & Smart Glasses:** Citizens carry screenless communication devices (Nexus Units) paired with Smart Glasses that project augmented reality (AR) overlays, navigation data, and real-time speech translation.
- **Micro-Chip ID & AR First-Name Display:** Adult citizens carry hand-implanted micro-chips for identification and health tracking. Smart Glasses project each person's verified first name in AR within 5 meters to foster polite interaction.
- **Mech-Animals:** Animal-shaped robots handle public safety, including Mech-dogs (patrols), Mech-hawks (aerial watch), and micro insect drones like Mech-roaches (surveillance) and Mech-bees (defense).
- **Kassen Sport & Narrative Happiness:** A popular, non-lethal sport simulating castle warfare, featuring 50:50 gender ratios and 3-meter-tall robotic umpires (Mech-giraffes). Symbolic leader Kasga inspires citizens to pursue "Narrative Happiness"—building meaningful personal life stories rather than accumulating financial wealth.

#### **9. Political Factions & Threat Matrix**

- **Hanafolk vs. "Rusty-Believers":** Citizens ("Hanafolk") embrace AI governance as a peaceful social evolution, while external anti-AI groups ("Rusty-Believers") condemn Hanasaka as a "Digital Dystopia" where humans live like dependent pets.
- **Pro-Mayor Faction vs. Castle Office:** Traditional political leaders in the City Office who favor human authority conflict with Castle Office (which manages Kassen and Castle Park) over Kasga's immense social influence.
- **Hostile External Entities:**
    - **Stone Cold:** A dangerous criminal generative AI operating on a "Machino-supremacy" ideology that treats humans as mindless tools or switches.
    - **Awakeners:** A violent anti-AI religious cult targeting Kasga and city facilities.
    - **Vaminas:** A violent underground criminal syndicate supplying illegal weapons and contraband to hostile factions.
